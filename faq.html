Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a session with TBfotography.

📷 How do I book a photography session? +

Click the Book Now button on your preferred package. You'll be redirected to WhatsApp where we'll confirm your booking and available dates.

👶 What ages do you photograph? +

We photograph babies, kids, adults, couples, families, graduation sessions, and special events.

💳 How do I make payment? +

Payment details will be shared with you during the booking process on WhatsApp.

📍 Where are you located? +

Our studio location will be shared once your booking has been confirmed.

⏰ How long does a session take? +

Most photography sessions last between 45 minutes and 2 hours, depending on the selected package.

🖼️ How many edited photos will I receive? +

The number of edited photos depends on the package you choose. Bronze, Silver and Gold packages all include different quantities.

📱 How will I receive my photos? +

Your edited photographs will be delivered digitally. Printed copies can also be discussed during booking if available.

❌ Can I reschedule my session? +

Yes. Please notify us as early as possible so we can arrange another suitable date.

🎉 Do you cover events? +

Yes. We cover weddings, birthdays, naming ceremonies, graduations, church programs, school events and many other occasions.
